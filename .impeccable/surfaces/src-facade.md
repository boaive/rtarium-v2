# Façade & Cladding (overview + three systems)

Scope: `/facade-cladding`, `/facade-cladding/unitized-thin-concrete-facade`, `/facade-cladding/rainscreen-cladding`,
`/facade-cladding/stud-frame-cladding`. Mode: Persuade with a Read core (specifiers must understand an assembly).
Visitors: architects, façade consultants, engineers, contractors, developers; many do not know TRC.
Job: understand what RTARIUM does inside a façade panel, then how each system is built and mounted.
Proof on hand: the production shoot (mould, face coat, mesh bent to the mould with ferrule inserts, backing coat,
demoulded panel, steel channels bolted to cast-in ferrules), the eye-bolt clip, the Blender mesh cell.
Constraints: homepage untouched; global nav untouched (a façade sub-navigation lives on these pages only);
no claim the client material does not support (90 % rain deflection, wind, thermal, fire, weight figures stay flagged).

## Direction contract

THESIS: one thin panel, three ways onto a building. Each page shows the real assembly in 3D, component by
component, in the order the client lists it; refuses the category's photo-hero, benefit cards and spec grids.
OWN-WORLD: the V2 world unchanged: yellow / black / warm-white grounds that own whole sections, Archivo 700 at
-0.04em for statements, Anybody only for the name, square-cut controls, basalt-bronze mesh, grey concrete,
galvanized steel, numbered leaders on clear ground, no decoration around the 3D.
STORY: mesh, then panel, then system; the visitor learns what each component is and where it sits, then moves
to the next system or back to the overview.
FIRST VIEWPORT: overview: the name of the section and the positioning line top left, a cut-away corner of a thin
panel with its return lower right, the mesh exposed at the cut, three short leaders; system pages: the finished
assembly isolated on the ground with the system name and one line in the clear corner opposite.
FORM: sticky 3D stage with full-screen steps whose copy moves corner to corner per step (homepage grammar), plus
per-page distinct blocks: pain/answer ledger and production photos (unitized), 3D section through the cavity and a
panel-length elevation (rainscreen), section drawing and numbered casting photographs (stud frame). seed: none (brief-pinned
world and structure, no roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
