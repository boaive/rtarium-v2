## Product Purpose

RTARIUM is a three-dimensional basalt reinforcement mesh for thin, high-performance concrete, by HEMISPHERE ONE. The
homepage must let a first-time visitor understand what it is, what is distinctive about its geometry, how the
technology is structured, and where it is used (flooring, cladding and façade, wall panels).

## Positioning

A 3D woven spacer structure: two parallel high-strength basalt mesh faces, completely integrated and held apart by
connecting fibres, so concrete fills the cavity and locks around the reinforcement in three dimensions. It is used as
an intermediate reinforcement replacing glass fibre (loose strands or 2D mat) and steel fibre in high-performance
concrete. Terminology: use "3D spacer structure" / "3D woven spacer". "Diamondcomb" is not a documented term and
must not be published as fact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed figures (client technical PDF; the margin notes are treated as corrections, owner decision October 2026):

- Mesh: 3D woven spacer reinforcement; sheet lengths 2.4 / 3.4 / 5.8 m; width 1,220 mm; thickness options
  3 / 6 / 10 / 15 mm; surface density 220 ±10 to 320 ±10 g/m²; tensile strength > 50 kN/m; elastic modulus
  79,600 MPa; elongation at break 3.4 %; sustained temperature resistance 820 °C; melting point 1,460 – 1,500 °C;
  non-corrosive; high alkali resistance.
- Impregnated yarn: tensile strength 2.62 GPa (structural steel 550 MPa, high-strength aircraft aluminium 570 MPa,
  titanium alloy 1,200 MPa, carbon fibre 3,500 MPa); elastic modulus 79.6 GPa (E-glass 72, AR-glass 74 GPa);
  elongation at break 3.40 % (glass fibre 2.5 %, steel fibre 1.5 %).
- RTARIUM reinforced concrete: axial tensile strength 19.7 MPa (~20; normal concrete 2 – 5 MPa); elastic-limit
  tensile strength 18.3 MPa; tensile elastic modulus 64 GPa (20 mm GFRC ~25 GPa, UHPC ~50 GPa); steel fibres in UHPC
  swapped for RTARIUM: tensile 16.2 → 20.9 MPa (+29 %), flexural 28.6 → 50.6 MPa (+77 %).
- Open: the two tensile figures (19.7 vs 20.9 MPa) may be two tests; no test standard, specimen, lab or mesh option
  is given for the concrete results; density is not tied to a thickness; "impregnated" yarn vs "no chemical
  top-coatings". Keep these flagged, not resolved.

## Product Principles

1. The mesh is the subject: every screen shows or explains the real geometry.
2. One message per screen, presentation pace: few scrolls, no waiting.
3. Figures only from the client's material, labelled by what they describe (mesh, yarn, concrete).
4. Explain in plain language first, then give the exact figure.
5. Claims the documents do not support are flagged, not published.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WebGL is unavailable.
